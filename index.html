About Us

Protecting crops with AI

Plant diseases cost farmers a large share of their harvest every year. Our project makes expert-level screening available to anyone with a phone camera.

🎯

Our purpose

Give farmers, gardeners and students a fast, free first check of leaf health and clear next steps.

🧠

How AI helps

A deep-learning image model (typically a CNN) is trained on thousands of labelled leaf photos. It learns patterns such as spots, mould and discolouration, then matches new leaves to known diseases.

🚜

Support for farmers

Early detection means timely treatment, fewer chemicals, lower losses and better yields.

🎓

Learning

Students can explore how computer vision solves real agricultural problems.

Solutions & Prevention

Keep plants healthy

🛡️

Prevent disease

Use certified disease-free seed, rotate crops, remove infected leaves, and clean tools between plants.

🚿

Water properly

Water at the base in the morning. Avoid wetting leaves and avoid standing water, since damp foliage encourages fungi.

🪱

Soil care

Add compost, ensure good drainage, test soil pH and nutrients, and mulch to limit soil splash.

🌾

Healthy practices

Give plants space for airflow, scout fields weekly, and choose resistant varieties.

👨‍🌾

Call an expert when…

Disease spreads fast, many plants are affected, you are unsure of the cause, or before using any pesticide. Contact your local agricultural extension office.
